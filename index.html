Building the Kingdom's financial planning profession. A balance sheet for every household.

FPNP, the Financial Planning Network Project, is a Saudi company in Riyadh building the Kingdom's financial planning profession: the Saudi standards the planner works to, the code that binds him, the training and examination before entry, the continuing development after it, and the public register where his name is recorded and checked. The work starts from a measured reading of the market. Savings grow in value, not in tenor. The household meets a seller of products or a reviewer of money already committed; nobody plans. The aim is a balance sheet and a systematic cash flow for every individual and household. The plan before the product. Wafi Alsalimi, Founder and Chief Executive. Raed Alharbi, Co-Founder and Chief Operating Officer. info@fpnp.sa
